blendboxd

Film picks your whole group will enjoy, blended from everyone's Letterboxd export.

1TMDb API key

blendboxd looks films up on TMDb, which needs a free API key. Ask whoever sent you this page for theirs, or get your own: make a free account on themoviedb.org, then Settings → API and copy the API Key (32 characters).

2Letterboxd exports

One export per person. To get theirs, everyone:

  1. Signs in on letterboxd.com in a browser (the phone app can't export).
  2. Opens letterboxd.com/settings/data and clicks Export your data. A .zip downloads.
  3. Sends you that .zip, unopened.

Put all the zips in one folder, then pick that folder. (Your own export counts too.)

or drop the zips here

      Your exports stay on this device: they are read here, in your browser, and never uploaded. Only film titles and years go to TMDb, to look the films up. Worth knowing when you pass zips around: an export also holds that person's email address. blendboxd only reads their username.

      Blending…

      The first blend takes a few minutes: every film gets looked up on TMDb once. The results are cached in this browser, so later blends take seconds.
